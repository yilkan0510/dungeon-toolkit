Formula

XP per upgrade is min(25,000,000, step × (level − offset)). Gold per upgrade is min(15,000, round(a·L² + b·L + c)).

The gold curve below the cap is an estimate fitted to game data, not an official formula. Compare a few levels in-game and adjust if needed. Your changes are saved in this browser.
